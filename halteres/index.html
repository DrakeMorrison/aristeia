SETTINGS

The practice
Simple & Sinister: 100 one-hand swings (10 sets of 10) and 10 Turkish get-ups (5 per side), most days, without the clock. Progress by step-loading in pairs — move two sets at a time to the next bell (2, then 4, then 6…) until all ten are there, then it becomes your bell. After four weeks at the same load with at least 70% of days practiced, the app nudges you to add the next pair. Test rarely: 100 swings in 5:00 and 10 get-ups in 10:00, same bell, is the standard.
