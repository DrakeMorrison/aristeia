    Paste a JSON array of cards, or a {"cards": [...]} object. Each card needs title and y (negative for BCE); m, d, c (circa), region, deck, blurb and details ([{"q", "a"}]) are optional. Cards already present are skipped.

    SETTINGS

    How it works
    Each card holds two memories, scheduled separately: when (graded by where you placed it: right gap = good, one gap off and within 25 years = hard, otherwise again) and what (the detail question, graded by you). Intervals grow SM-2 style. A card is due when either memory is. Runs only reschedule memories that are due or new, but a miss always counts.

    Beeminder
    Posts a datapoint per finished session, straight from this browser to beeminder.com. Deleting a session zeroes its datapoint; offline sessions queue until you're back. Get your token at beeminder.com/api/v1/auth_token.json (it stays in this browser only). Leave any field empty to disable.
    Data
    Everything is stored in this browser only. JSON holds your own cards, all progress and the session log (not the Beeminder token); importing one merges it in. CSV is the session log, for analysis elsewhere.
